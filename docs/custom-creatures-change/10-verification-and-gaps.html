Known gaps

Not re-run after the visual rebuild: a real OS drag/drop gesture and successful system clipboard write. Both paths are outside the changed CSS/render/control construction, and their parser/fallback logic remains covered or unchanged, but this browser pass did not claim those two integrations.

  • The Clipboard fallback is code-reviewed and was covered in the original feature verification, but this pass did not force a denied clipboard permission.
  • Only the bundled single-creature manifest was available, so automatic rotation between multiple featured entries was not visually replayed. Its pause rules are unchanged.
  • Screenshots are intentionally ignored scratch artifacts, not committed binary documentation.

Review conclusion

The rebuild changes presentation boundaries, canvas background treatment and component reuse without changing manifest, validation, storage or simulation semantics. Automated tests and browser flow checks agree with that boundary.
